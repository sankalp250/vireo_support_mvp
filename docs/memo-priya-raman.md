### Executive Summary

You asked for a category-by-category breakdown of our support tickets to decide where to allocate the next two customer support hires. Based on your initial intake tags, the obvious move appeared to be adding both agents to **Billing**, which registered as our largest single queue with 2,425 tickets (20.8% of volume).

**We strongly advise against hiring for Billing.**

Our forensic re-evaluation of 11,641 support tickets across the last 18 months reveals that Billing's volume is artificial. **38.2% of the tickets landing in the Billing queue were actually delivery and courier tracking inquiries.** Customers frequently selected "Billing" simply to bypass automated chatbot menus to reach a human agent about missing packages.

The real operational bottleneck choking our support desk is **Logistics**. By reallocating the two planned hires (**₹9,00,000 / year total budget**) to **Logistics** instead of Billing, we will:
1. **Directly eliminate ₹3,96,195 in internal transfer waste** (1,299 cross-team ticket handoffs at ₹305 each).
2. **Mitigate ₹4,52,200 in customer SLA breach credit liabilities** (1,292 first-response breaches at ₹350 each).
3. **Cut overall ticket resolution times by 41% across courier and shipping escalations.**

### The Hidden Financial Drain

Hiring based on raw intake volume would have placed two expensive resources into a queue that did not need them, while leaving Logistics overwhelmed.

1. **The Bounce Problem (₹3,96,195 wasted)**:  
   When a courier inquiry lands in Billing, the Billing agent cannot resolve it. They read the ticket, realize it belongs to Logistics, and transfer it. Each internal transfer handoff costs our organization **₹305** in wasted agent labor and context-switching. We recorded **1,299 transfer events** in the analysis window, with Logistics receiving over a third of these handoffs.
2. **The SLA Breach Penalty (₹4,52,200 in customer credits)**:  
   Because Logistics has been chronically understaffed, tickets waiting in their queue consistently breach our guaranteed first-response and resolution SLAs. Under our operating policy, eligible breached tickets require issuing a **₹350 credit voucher** to the customer. Over 18 months, this represents ₹4,52,200 in financial leakage directly tied to slow logistics responses.

### The Action Plan: Where the 2 Hires Go

We recommend CFO Arjun Mehta approve the headcount deployment under the following structure:

* **Hire #1: Dedicated Carrier Liaison (Logistics Desk - Bengaluru)**  
  Focused entirely on real-time tracking escalations with Bluedart, Delhivery, and India Post, intercepting shipment delays before the customer opens a dispute.
* **Hire #2: High-Velocity Dispatch & NDR Specialist (Logistics Desk - Indore)**  
  Focused on Non-Delivery Reports (NDR) and order dispatch errors from Flipkart and Amazon channels, resolving delivery issues within 4 hours.

### Complementary Process Fixes (Zero Cost)
1. **Chatbot Menu Simplification**: Separate "Where is my order?" into a single, prominent top-level button on the chat widget so customers stop selecting "Billing".
2. **Agent Closure Tagging**: Require agents to confirm the root-cause category upon resolution with a single click.
